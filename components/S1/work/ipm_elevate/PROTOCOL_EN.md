# Paper M IPM revision 02 evaluation protocol

The published historical lineage and all previous cohort hashes, thresholds and outcomes remain part of the record. This follow-up analyses the selection implications of the exact paired predicate, adds a size sensitivity analysis and, where structurally feasible, evaluates a separate naturally incomplete TREC cohort. The DL19, DL20 and DL21 cohorts have previously been analysed and all new outcomes on them are follow-up evidence. Synthetic and controlled cases are reported separately. A new dataset is external to development when its run list, case construction and outcome rules are frozen before outcome inspection. TREC editions are not independent replications of a benchmark family.

## Paired outcomes

The primary endpoint is top-three membership of four fixed run outputs at depth ten. The second endpoint is the ordered top-three prefix. Preserve lexical tie-breaking, binary grade threshold 2, linear grade score and the single shared grade per unjudged query–passage pair. For fixed depth, normalising each score by its arm-specific denominator preserves order. Report naturally incomplete, fully observed and controlled masked cases separately. Case-specific completion counts are combinatorial and supply no probability weights for the research conclusion.

For each endpoint, classify whether at least one common completion agrees and whether at least one disagrees. Classify output change at the common all-zero completion and compare it to existential disagreement. An all-zero disagreement is an exact change witness in this domain. An all-zero agreement leaves the universal decision open. Count the cases in which these conclusions differ and verify representative counterexamples directly. Add the pointwise all-three comparison as a second point screen. Compare pointwise equivalence with exact equality of the two marginal sets of possible outputs. Equal marginal sets do not in general identify pointwise agreement. Two separate completions allow a discrepancy unless both marginal sets are the same singleton. Test both shortcuts on the same outputs and explicitly report all zero cells.

For the secondary k-sensitivity analysis, enumerate k = 1, 2, 3, keeping the four runs fixed. Separate membership and prefix, which coincide when k = 1. Report strict membership disagreements only where both cuts have positive score gaps, and strict prefix disagreements only where within-prefix gaps and the cut are positive under both codings. This extends the empirical adjudication. The existing portable proof and its timing threshold remain specific to k = 3.

For natural sharing, report the number of unjudged pairs present in more than one of the four runs and the query denominator. Additionally test whether a change indicator can vary by the grade of one naturally shared passage while every other grade is held fixed. An exact search over the other grades can establish relevance to the verdict. This is an existence diagnostic, not a human study or a frequency of deployed use.

## Exact enumeration

Use compressed contribution states by grouping passages with identical incidence across the four runs. Per group retain every reachable binary count and grade sum with multiplicity and one canonical representative assignment. Combine the groups and evaluate both codings on the same state. Identical incidence makes each group's two totals sufficient, so state compression preserves the possible paired outputs, the strict boundary predicates and completion cardinalities. An all-runs incidence group cancels from every pair difference and may be omitted if its completion multiplicity is restored. Compare the primary endpoints to the frozen independent references where available and test compressed enumeration against direct assignment on small cases. For audit summaries do not publish source runs, raw labels or individual query records without rights confirmation.

Prespecified cap for exact enumeration is 2,000,000 compressed states per query, or per natural sharing subsearch. Mark an uncompleted query or pivotality query as unresolved at the corresponding stage and keep it in the coverage denominator. For a new cohort, apply the same procedure to all structurally eligible queries. Retain source hash, non-overlap with development qids, run provenance and structural coverage, regardless of result. Re-run the portable checker for the primary endpoints on the new corpus. Keep previously frozen DL21 latency criterion and result unchanged. Evidence handoff and a generic certificate comparison are separate analyses and cannot substitute for that result.
