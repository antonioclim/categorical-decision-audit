# Distribution history

## 2.0.0 — prepared 12 November 2026

This distribution supplies the reproducibility materials for the revised
article *When Relevance Coding Changes the Shortlist: Exact evidence for
retrieval run selection under shared incomplete judgements* (R07-D2).

- S1 contains the original flat-score retrieval audit.
- S2 contains the quantised position-discount extension, independent checking
  and enumeration, aggregate expectations and three editable figures.
- All 96 files in S1 and S2 are byte-identical to the validated R07-D2 companion
  components. This distribution change only updates the outer documentation,
  citation, rights/status records and file manifest, and adds this history.
- There is no claim that these retrieval experiments reproduce the earlier
  Car Evaluation or Wine Quality analyses. The empirical scope has changed.
- No new empirical computation or new statistical interpretation is introduced
  by assembling this distribution. Retained limitations and negative results
  remain in the scientific components.

At preparation, the v2.0.0 tag did not exist and no new DOI had been confirmed.
Distribution version 2.0.0 is distinct from research version R07 and editorial
revision R07-D2.
