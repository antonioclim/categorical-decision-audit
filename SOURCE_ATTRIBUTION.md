# Source attribution

## Included datasets

Bohanec, M. (1988). *Car Evaluation* [Dataset]. UCI Machine Learning Repository. https://doi.org/10.24432/C5JP48

Cortez, P., Cerdeira, A., Almeida, F., Matos, T., & Reis, J. (2009). *Wine Quality* [Dataset]. UCI Machine Learning Repository. https://doi.org/10.24432/C56S3T

Both datasets and the identified adaptations retain [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) sets out the file-level scope. Source receipts bind the retained archives and CSVs to their SHA-256 digests.

## Associated source article

Cortez, P., Cerdeira, A., Almeida, F., Matos, T., & Reis, J. (2009). Modeling wine preferences by data mining from physicochemical properties. *Decision Support Systems, 47*(4), 547–553. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dss.2009.05.016

The article is cited for attribution; no copy is included.

## Other source records

EPA and EPSS source identifiers, URLs, snapshot dates and expected byte digests appear in [data/upstream_inputs.json](data/upstream_inputs.json). Their original exports are not bundled. Secondary EPSS exports come from [EPSS-Compare](https://github.com/jgamblin/EPSS-Compare); the [FIRST EPSS Special Interest Group](https://www.first.org/epss/faq) maintains EPSS and Empirical Security generates its scores.

[data/origin_manifest.json](data/origin_manifest.json) records the archive and member digests from which the retained study projections were drawn. References to excluded archives identify provenance and do not include or license those archives.
