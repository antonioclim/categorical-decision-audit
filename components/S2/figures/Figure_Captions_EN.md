Joint attainable membership outcomes for DL22 query 2017299 under flat scoring.** Rows and columns identify the omitted run under the binary and linear profiles; each omitted run uniquely determines the top-three membership set. **Figure 2. Possible, strict and zero-detected changes in the 70 naturally incomplete DL22 cases.** The panels compare top-three membership and ordered prefix under the flat rule and the discounted rule at scale $10^6$. A strict membership change has positive cut-off gaps in both profiles; the strict prefix test requires all adjacent gaps to be positive. The plotted categories overlap and are not components of a partition.

**Figure 3. Pointwise sensitivity to the quantisation scale in DL22.** Each comparison evaluates the same shared completion at two scales. Affected-case counts refer to cases with a change in at least one profile's full order; completion counts refer to changes in the binary-versus-graded disagreement indicator for membership or prefix. Existence verdicts per case remain unchanged across the three scales. The two counting units are shown separately and are not probabilities.
